# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives and ideas.
- Give helpful and honest feedback.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Respect the project's maintainers and contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, threats, trolling, or any form of abusive behavior will not be tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or access from anyone who violates this Code of Conduct.

---

Thank you for helping keep this project respectful and welcoming.
